A Hidden Gem on the Eastern Bypass

Tucked away off Eastern Bypass, Amila Suites and Lounge was built with one principle: that a hotel should feel like a reward. Not a transaction. From the first step inside, the outside world softens.

02
Your Privacy Is Sacred Here

Our guests trust us with their most important moments — anniversaries, retreats, and quiet mornings that matter. We have built our reputation on discretion. What happens at Amila, stays at Amila.

03
The Sunset That Stops Time

Every evening, the Nairobi skyline turns to copper and amber. Our lounge is positioned to catch every second of it. Your drink gets colder. The conversation gets warmer. The city below does its thing. You get to just breathe.

Our Story

Bold. Serene.
Unapologetically Nairobi.

Amila Suites and Lounge was born from a simple belief: that Nairobi deserved a space that felt both bold and calm. A place where the city's energy is visible from a terrace but cannot find its way inside.

We sit on Eastern Bypass — close enough to everything, far enough from the noise. Our suites are designed for guests who know the difference between a room and a sanctuary.
